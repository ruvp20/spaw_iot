# Mechanical Specifications & Assembly

## 1. Load Cell Assembly
```text
           [ Pet Feeding Bowl ]
                    |
           [ Platform Plate A ]
                    |
              M4 Bolt Top
                    |
         [==== 3kg Load Cell ====]
              (Strain Gauge)
                    |
             M5 Bolt Bottom
                    |
           [ Platform Plate B ]
                    |
              [ Rigid Base ]
```
* **Critical Rule:** The load cell must be mounted as a cantilever beam. Plate A attaches ONLY to one end of the load cell (top hole), and Plate B attaches ONLY to the opposite end (bottom hole). The bowl must NEVER touch the feeder frame or chute directly, or weight readings will bypass the strain gauge.

## 2. Dispenser Gate & Anti-Jam
* **Flap Construction:** The flap connects directly to the MG996R horn via a linkage rod or direct axle.
* **Silicone Edge:** The leading edge of the gate is lined with a 2mm flexible food-safe silicone strip. If a kibble gets caught between the closing gate and the PVC pipe wall, the flexible edge flexes and pushes the kibble outward rather than seizing the servo motor gears.
